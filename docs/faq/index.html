FAQ

Clear answers for store review

These answers describe the current extension behavior conservatively. The extension supports selected AI sites only and does not bypass site limits.

以下回答保守描述当前插件行为。插件只支持指定 AI 网站，不绕过目标网站限制。

English

Why are clipboard permissions needed?

`clipboardRead` is needed to read the screenshot you copied after you trigger the extension or enable automatic mode. For a manual Gemini or Doubao action, `clipboardWrite` may prepare the captured image immediately before a browser paste; it also supports the optional failure fallback. Automatic mode does not use the real-paste path.

简体中文

为什么需要剪贴板权限？

`clipboardRead` 用于在你触发插件或开启自动模式后读取刚复制的截图。手动附加到 Gemini 或豆包时， `clipboardWrite` 可能会在浏览器粘贴前准备本次捕获的图片，也用于可选的失败回退。自动模式不使用真实粘贴路径。

English

Why are site permissions needed?

Site permissions allow the extension to open, focus, and inject the attachment runtime into supported AI pages only: ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Doubao. The extension does not request access to all websites.

简体中文

为什么需要站点权限？

站点权限让插件能够打开、聚焦并向受支持 AI 页面注入附加运行时代码。范围仅限 ChatGPT、Claude、Gemini 和豆包，不请求访问所有网站。

English

Why does the extension not send messages automatically?

Sending stays manual so you can review the attachment, edit your prompt, and decide what to share with the target AI site. This also avoids accidental submission of private screenshots.

简体中文

为什么插件不自动发送消息？

发送保持手动，这样你可以检查附件、修改提示词，并决定是否分享给目标 AI 网站，避免误发私密截图。

English

What happens when attachment fails?

Some AI sites reject synthetic paste, drop, or file-input actions. When attachment fails, the extension can keep the screenshot on the clipboard and focus the input so you can paste manually.

简体中文

附加失败时会发生什么？

一些 AI 网站会拒绝合成 paste、drop 或 file-input 操作。附加失败时，插件可以把截图保留在剪贴板，并聚焦输入框， 方便你手动粘贴。
